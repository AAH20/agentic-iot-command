Facility sizing, lifecycle cost & unit economics

Demand envelope → facility capacity and space → classified quantity takeoff → dated source prices → lifecycle cash flow → actual-cost reconciliation → service unit economics. IFC/BIM element IDs can enrich the takeoff; they are not treated as a complete quantity source.

Price-source discipline: every price needs geography, currency, unit, source reference, observed/valid dates, delivery basis, confidence and approval state. Estimates without a supported quantity or price remain explicitly unpriced. No synthetic value is presented as a vendor quote or tariff.
